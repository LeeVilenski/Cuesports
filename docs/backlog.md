# Backlog

Things noted during playtesting to come back to. Newest first.

- **Messages and sheets cover too much of the table.** Shot messages, the scoreboard and the push-out and
  rack sheets sit over the playing surface, especially in portrait. They should be smaller, sit at the edges
  or fade quickly, and never cover the cue ball, the object ball or the pocket being played. (Noted after
  M3.)
- **Snooker and English pool pockets play far too easy.** They need their own rounded "cut" pocket model.
  Planned for M4.
- **"Hand the phone over" screen** between turns in local two-player matches.
- **9-ball:** break-box option; fouls that need tracking the cue ball in hand (touching a ball, double hits).
